7 Revenue Leaks Silently Draining Your Home Service Business

We've analyzed over 800 home service businesses from Tampa Bay to Atlanta. Here's what's bleeding your revenue every single day — and exactly how much it's costing you:

  • ✗ Missed Calls After Hours & During Jobs: 63% of callers never call back — each missed call in Florida costs a home service business an average of $340–$1,200 in lost revenue depending on trade. Your competitor answers 24/7 with AI. Do you?
  • ✗ Slow Follow-Up Killing Your Conversion Rate: Leads not contacted within 5 minutes are 21× less likely to close. Most home service teams follow up in hours — or not at all. You're paying for leads you're giving away free.
  • ✗ Weak Reputation Handing Jobs to Competitors: 87% of buyers check reviews before hiring. If your Google profile has fewer than 50 reviews, you're losing to someone who has 200. In Hillsborough and Pinellas counties alone, reputation gaps cost businesses $3,000–$15,000/month in bid losses.
  • ✗ No Follow-Up Automation — Leads Die in Limbo: The average home service business has 47% of its leads that were never properly followed up on sitting in a CRM or a spreadsheet. Those are booked jobs waiting to happen — if someone contacts them.
  • ✗ Tool Overload — 7 Apps, None Talking to Each Other: CRM here, scheduling there, reviews somewhere else, invoicing in another tab. The average home service company wastes 14 hours/week on manual data entry between disconnected systems.
  • ✗ Wasted Ad Spend — Pouring Water Into a Leaky Bucket: Most agencies will happily run your Google Ads before fixing your follow-up or answering system. 50–70% of paid leads go to voicemail and never convert. We fix the bucket before we pour in a single dollar of ad spend.
  • ✗ No Content, No Authority, No Inbound Leads: If you're not showing up on Google when someone in Pasco, Polk, or Manatee County types your trade + city, you're invisible to 82% of your potential market. Content authority takes 90 days to build — the time to start was 3 months ago.
